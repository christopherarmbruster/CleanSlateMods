S197 Mustang GT Fog Light Grille Delete

Transform your 2010+ Mustang GT in minutes.

Get Yours Today

Designed for the S197 platform, these grilles fit 2010 and newer Mustang GT models with center fog lights. We ran these on our own race car to aid in cooling through the radiator for race applications. After testing many material types, we found this carbon fiber impregnated plastic was the best choice for 3D printing durability. They are clean, sleek, and incredibly easy to install.

Installation Instructions

  • Remove the 3 screws holding each existing fog light.
  • Pop our grilles into the empty housing slots.
  • Apply three drops of superglue to secure in place. Done!
